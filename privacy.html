Good Years Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Good Years is designed so that we cannot see your data — because it never leaves your device.

What we collect: nothing

Good Years does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app works entirely offline. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Your answers stay on your device

The lifestyle answers you enter are stored only in the app's local storage on your device so the app can show your results. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app permanently deletes them, as does "Delete everything" in the app's settings. Good Years does not read from or write to Apple Health (HealthKit).

Purchases

The one-time purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no personal information about you from that transaction.

Children

Good Years is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy label before release.
