What Good Years is — and is not

Good Years is an educational wellness app. It shows statistical estimates based on population data — averages across large groups of people, drawn from published life tables and peer-reviewed cohort studies. These estimates are never a prediction about you as an individual. Your actual health and lifespan depend on many factors no app can know, including genetics, medical history, and chance.

The app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, screening, or treatment, and it is not a substitute for professional care. For decisions about your health, talk to a doctor or other qualified health professional. Never disregard professional medical advice, or delay seeking it, because of something shown in this app.
